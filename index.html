About Campus Life Planner

Campus Life Planner is an app that helps students keep their school life organized. It puts classes, homework deadlines, exams, clubs, and events all in one calendar and sends reminders so nothing is forgotten. Students can also set goals, build healthy habits, and share calendars with friends for group projects. It even shows useful campus information, like dining hall hours and library room bookings. This way, students feel less stress and have more time to enjoy college.
